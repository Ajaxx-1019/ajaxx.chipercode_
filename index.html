About

About this web

Secret Cipher is a small toolbox for hiding, reading and recognising coded messages. Everything runs in your browser: your text, audio and microphone never leave your device.

Notices

Signal Lab is newEight audio modes (ABP, HZalpha, Morse, WaveSig, AcDense, HyperDense, FSK, DTMF). Type a message, play it, save it as WAV, or decode a file or microphone recording.
Cipher detectorPaste any text in the Cipher tab and it shows the most likely ciphers with a percentage. Short texts are harder to judge, so the percentage drops.
New navigationUse the bar at the bottom. Tap a tab, or drag the glass bubble between tabs.
Steganography is hereHide text or a file inside an image, a WAV sound, plain text or any file. A password is optional. The Change log shows exactly which colors were changed. The third tab is still reserved.
Signal modes are customAcDense, HyperDense, WaveSig and ABP are defined by this app. Audio made here is meant to be decoded here.

Pixel Wall

One shared canvas of 100 x 100 pixels. Everyone paints on the same wall, and each person gets 25 pixels per minute, plus a brush, 3 star bombs a day and an undo. The wall is wiped every day at 00:00 WIB (Jakarta time), so it starts empty again.
